Community Guidelines

WE ARE. exists so people who feel different can find others who understand them. That only works if the community feels genuinely safe — these guidelines describe what we expect from everyone here, and what happens if they're not followed.

The spirit of it

Connect through who you are and what you care about. Disagree honestly, but treat people with respect. Assume good faith. If a conversation stops feeling like that, it's fine to walk away from it — you're never obligated to keep engaging with someone.

Never allowed, no exceptions

  • Sexual content involving minors, sexual solicitation, grooming, or any sexual exploitation of a minor.
  • Sexual harassment, threats, or coercion directed at anyone.
  • Explicit sexual content or inappropriate sexual communication directed at a minor.
  • Harassment, targeted abuse, or credible threats of violence.
  • Hate speech or content that attacks people based on who they are.
  • Impersonating another person, or creating a fake account to deceive people.
  • Spam, scams, or coordinated inauthentic behavior.

Age and safety

WE ARE. includes both teenagers and adults, and the platform is built to keep those groups appropriately separated by default — see our Privacy Policy for how age is handled. Adult-only content is never shown to a minor. If you believe a minor is being targeted or endangered anywhere on the platform, report it immediately or reach us directly through Contact — reports involving a minor's safety are treated as our highest priority.

Reporting and moderation

Every post, comment, and profile can be reported. Reports go to a human moderator, who can warn a user, remove content, or suspend or ban an account, depending on severity. We keep an internal log of every moderation action taken. If you think a decision about your account was wrong, you can appeal it by reaching out through our Contact page.

Because private messages are intended to be end-to-end encrypted once that work is complete, moderators can't see message content by default. If something in a private conversation needs to be reported, you can voluntarily submit it as evidence when you file the report.

Follow, Connect, and Block

Following someone doesn't obligate them to follow back, and connecting requires mutual acceptance — nobody can message you privately until you've accepted a connection. You can block anyone at any time, for any reason, with no explanation owed.

Changes

We may update these guidelines as the community grows. Meaningful changes will be announced, not made silently.
